Professional outreach

Reach the people who meet two-home families right when things change: family law attorneys, mediators, child and family therapists, school counselors and co-parenting class teachers. One yes from them reaches a steady stream of families.

Start here

Begin with people you already know

A warm introduction gets a yes far more often than a cold package. Before anyone on the list below, write down the professionals already in your circle:

  • The family law attorney or mediator from Kirsten's own separation, if there was one.
  • Sailor's school counselor, or a therapist she or the family has seen.
  • Friends and school parents who are therapists, attorneys, mediators or counselors.
  • Anyone who has already bought or gifted a 2Homies. Ask who they'd pass it to.

Add each one to the tracker at the bottom of this page with the kind School or whichever fits.

How to reach out

Email first, then mail

For people you know, skip the email and send or drop off the book. For everyone on the list, email first.

  • You get the right person and address. Packages to a law firm or group practice often stop at the front desk. The email tells you who should get it.
  • Books go to people who want one. Each copy costs you money. A yes by email means it gets opened and read.
  • No reply in a week? Mail the book anyway to your top 10, addressed to the person by name. Then follow up once.
  • Nearby offices (Venice, Santa Monica, Culver City): dropping off in person beats both. Bring a book and handouts.
