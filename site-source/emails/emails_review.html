Emails for review

Five emails. The first one works today inside Big Cartel. The next three need an email tool (like Kit or Mailchimp) connected to the shop. The pack email is one Kirsten sends herself.

Note: email apps don't show our hand-drawn font, so in the real emails the big headings will either be saved as images or show up in a bold standard font.

3. Your first week

Sent about 10 days after an order, so it lands once the book has arrived. This is the "how to use this calendar" email.

Your first week with 2Homies
From: Kirsten & Chris at 2Homies
Preview: Five small steps to get started.

Ready, set, color.

Your 2Homies should be home by now. The first page tells your kid how to use it. Here's the short version:

  • 1Color-code your days.Pick a color for each home so the week makes sense at a glance.
  • 2Add the fun things.Yes days, sleepovers, field trips. Write them in or use the stickers.
  • 3Don't forget the important stuff.Haircuts, doctor visits, project due dates.
  • 4For when feelings get big.Turn to the tools pages.
  • 5Have fun.Plans change and mistakes happen. That's OK.
Tip: Let your kid hold the pen. The more it's theirs, the more they'll use it.

We'd love to hear how it's going. Just hit reply.

Love, Kirsten & Christhe moms who made 2Homies
