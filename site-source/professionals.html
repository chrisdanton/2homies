How it fits your work

Something just for the kids.

Most of a separation is about the grown-ups. 2Homies gives kids a calendar they can read themselves, and pages that make room for feelings.

Attorneys and mediators

A thoughtful gift for clients with kids.

Hand it over when the parenting plan is set. Kids color-code each home and see the whole week at once, whatever the schedule: week on, week off, 2-2-3 or every other weekend.

Therapists and counselors

Tools kids can use between sessions.

A feelings wheel and star breathing give kids words for what's going on inside, and a way to calm down on their own. The feelings pages belong to the kid, so they decide what to share.

Co-parenting classes and coaches

One book that travels between homes.

The plan goes where the kid goes, so both homes see the same week. There's even a page that says "If you find this book, please contact one of my adults."

Our story

Made because nothing else existed.

Kirsten's daughter Sailor was getting overwhelmed. Who's picking her up from gymnastics? When is she switching homes? She was too young for a phone, and regular calendars weren't made for her life.

So Kirsten and her friend Chris made one that was. Maxine McCrann drew every page.
